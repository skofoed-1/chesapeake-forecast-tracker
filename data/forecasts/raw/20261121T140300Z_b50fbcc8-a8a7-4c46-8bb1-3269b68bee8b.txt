.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

High pressure initially over the area will begin to retreat back 
into New England and the Canadian Maritimes this weekend. Winds 
remain light today before a low pressure system emanating from the 
southeastern U.S. coast should bring precipitation and increased 
winds to the waters on Sunday. Small Craft Advisories may be needed 
at times Sunday into Sunday night with this coastal system nearby. 
Stronger winds will be possible, but there has been a recent trend 
to move the system further to the east. Additional advisories are 
likely to be needed on Tuesday into Wednesday in response to the 
next weather maker ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday.
