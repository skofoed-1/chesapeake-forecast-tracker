.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

High pressure will remain over the area throughout the entire  week.
The lighter winds support favorable conditions across the  marine
waters. A low pressure system is forecast to develop off the  coast
of Florida and may bring precipitation and increased winds to  the
waters on Sunday which may approach SCA criteria. SCAs likely by 
late Sunday night through Monday as the low pressure system rides up
 the east coast. Stronger winds will be possible, but depend on the 
exact track of the low.
