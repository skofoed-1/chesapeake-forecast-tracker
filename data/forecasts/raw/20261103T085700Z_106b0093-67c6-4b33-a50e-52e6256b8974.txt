.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

High pressure will meander offshore today before shifting further 
into the western Atlantic tonight. Low pressure approaches from the 
west Wednesday before passing near the waters on Friday. Weak high

crosses early next week. Winds may approach low-end Small Craft
