.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

A potent coastal low pressure system will approach from the south 
tonight, then move over or just east of the region on Thanksgiving 
Day. The low will pull away by Friday before reaching the Canadian 
Maritimes on Saturday. Weak high pressure builds over the eastern 
U.S. late in the weekend. Gale and storm conditions are expected at 
times from tonight through midday Friday. Additional Small Craft 
Advisories will likely be needed through midday Saturday.
