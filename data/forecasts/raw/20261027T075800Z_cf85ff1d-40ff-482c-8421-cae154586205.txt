.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

A weakening cold front will drop southward across the Mid-Atlantic 
into North Carolina tonight as high pressure builds over New 
England. The front will return northward as a warm front Wednesday. 
High pressure will then persist near and east of the region through 
the end of the week before another dry cold front moves through. 
Another area of high pressure will build from the Great Lakes to the
 Northeast through the weekend while a frontal system gradually 
approaches from the west.
