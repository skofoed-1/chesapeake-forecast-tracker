.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

High pressure will move offshore through the end of the week as a 
cold front approaches from the west. This front will stall near the 
area this weekend when a wave of low pressure may pass nearby or to 
the south. High pressure will likely return early next week. Small 
Craft Advisories may be needed at times in south to southwest flow 
Thursday evening through Friday afternoon, then in northerly flow 
Friday night into Saturday. Additional Small Craft Advisories are 
likely Monday into Tuesday.
