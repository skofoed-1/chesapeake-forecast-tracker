.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

A front near the North Carolina/Virginia border will return north as
 a warm front through tonight. High pressure will then persist near 
and east of the region through the end of the week before a dry cold
 front moves through late Friday. Behind the front Friday evening, 
gusty winds could necessitate Small Craft Advisories. Another area 
of high pressure will build from the Great Lakes to the Northeast 
through the weekend while a frontal system gradually approaches from
 the west.
