.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

A strong area of high pressure will remain in place to our north 
through much of the week leading to a prolonged period of onshore 
flow. Several waves of low pressure may track along a stalled front 
to our south during this time. Small Craft Advisories will likely be
 needed for much of the Wednesday through Friday time period, and a 
few low-end Gale gusts can't be ruled out.
