.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

High pressure over the central Appalachians will slowly drop toward 
the Tennessee Valley and weaken through tonight while a cold front 
approaches from the north. This front will come through dry Tuesday 
morning, followed by another large area of high pressure through the
