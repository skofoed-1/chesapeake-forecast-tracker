.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

High pressure remains positioned north of the area through the 
middle of the week as low pressure meanders off the Mid-Atlantic 
coast. The low will likely depart by the end of the week as high 
pressure builds overhead. Additional Small Craft Advisories will 
likely be needed at times through much of the week. Marginal gale- 
force gusts are also possible at times on the wider waters of the 
middle Chesapeake Bay.
