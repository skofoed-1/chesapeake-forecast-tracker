.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

Breezy conditions continue through tonight between low pressure to 
our northeast and approaching high pressure from the west. High 
pressure will build over the area Tuesday before meandering nearby 
for much of this coming week. Small Craft Advisories may need to be 
extended for lower portions of the Chesapeake Bay into Monday. Winds
