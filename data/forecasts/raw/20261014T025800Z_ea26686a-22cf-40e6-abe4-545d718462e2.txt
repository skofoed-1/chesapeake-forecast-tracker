.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

High pressure builds to the south of the waters through this evening
 before shifting offshore Wednesday morning. Southerly channeling 
could result in brief periods of Small Craft Advisory conditions. A 
dry cold front will push southward Wednesday afternoon into 
Wednesday night, then stall near or just south of the region on 
Thursday. Small Craft Advisories may be needed Wednesday night into 
Thursday. A stronger cold front moves across the area Friday, with 
gale conditions possible Friday night into Saturday.
