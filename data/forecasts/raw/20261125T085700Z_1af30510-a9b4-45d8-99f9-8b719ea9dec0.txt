.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

A potent coastal low pressure system will approach Wednesday night, 
then move over or just east of the region on Thanksgiving Day. The 
low will pull away by Friday before reaching the Canadian Maritimes 
on Saturday. Gale or even storm conditions are possible at times 
from late Wednesday night through midday Friday. Additional Small 
Craft Advisories will likely be needed through midday Saturday.
