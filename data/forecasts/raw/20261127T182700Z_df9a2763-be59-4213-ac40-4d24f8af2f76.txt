.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

A potent coastal low pressure system continues to pull away from the
 region today. Weak high pressure builds over the eastern U.S. late 
in the weekend before the next cold front crosses the waters by 
early Monday. Additional Small Craft Advisories will likely be 
needed through midday Saturday, and again Sunday evening into 
Monday. Gale conditions are likely Monday morning.
