.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

High pressure will move offshore through Friday as a cold front 
approaches from the west. This front will stall near the area this 
weekend when a wave of low pressure may pass to the south. A second 
cold front will cross the area on Monday. Small Craft Advisories may

Saturday. Additional Small Craft Advisories are likely Monday into 
Tuesday. No marine hazards are expected by the middle of next week.
