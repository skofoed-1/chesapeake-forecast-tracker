.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

A strong area of high pressure will build to our north  today and
remain in place through much of the week, leading to a  prolonged
period of onshore flow. Several waves of low pressure may  track
along the stalled front to our south during this time. Small  Craft
Advisories will likely be needed for much of the Wednesday  through
Friday time period, and a few low-end Gale gusts can't be  ruled out
Wednesday through Friday.
