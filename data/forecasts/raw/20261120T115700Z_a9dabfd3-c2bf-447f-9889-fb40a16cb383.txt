.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

High pressure will remain over the area through late tonight before 
the system pulls away toward the northeast. Continued light winds 
support favorable conditions across the marine waters. A low 
pressure system is forecast to develop off the coast of Florida and 
may bring precipitation and increased winds to the waters on Sunday 
which may approach SCA criteria. SCAs likely by late Sunday night 
through Monday as the low pressure system rides up the East Coast. 
Stronger winds will be possible, but will be dependent on the exact 
track of the low. SCA conditions likely continue into Tuesday as 
well.
