.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

Behind an earlier cold front, a strong area of Canadian high 
pressure will build to the north through the middle of the week. A 
potent coastal low pressure system will approach Wednesday night, 
then move over or just east of the region on Thanksgiving Day. The 
low will pull away by Friday before reaching the Canadian Maritimes 
on Saturday. Small Craft Advisories will likely be needed through 
Wednesday evening, with gale conditions possible at times from late 
Wednesday night through midday Friday. Thereafter, additional Small 
Craft Advisories are likely needed through midday Saturday.
