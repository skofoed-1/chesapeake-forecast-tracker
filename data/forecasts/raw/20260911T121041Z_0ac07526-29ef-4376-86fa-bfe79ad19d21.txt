.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

A slow moving cold front will cross the waters this morning before 
the boundary settles over the Carolinas on Saturday. The front 
returns northward as a warm front late in the weekend before another

early next week. Expect some Small Craft Advisory level channeling 
this upcoming weekend, mainly over the open waters.
