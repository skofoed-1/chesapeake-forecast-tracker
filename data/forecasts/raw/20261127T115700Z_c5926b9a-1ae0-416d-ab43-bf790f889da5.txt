.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

A potent coastal low pressure system continues to pull away from the
 region today. Gale conditions continue through this afternoon. Weak
 high pressure builds over the eastern U.S. late in the weekend 
before the next cold front crosses the waters by early Monday. 
Additional Small Craft Advisories will likely be needed through 
midday Saturday, and again Sunday evening into Monday. Near-Gale 
conditions are possible Monday morning.
