.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

A potent coastal low pressure system will impact the area through 
tonight. This system will pull away by Friday before reaching the 
Canadian Maritimes on Saturday. Weak high pressure builds over the 
eastern U.S. late in the weekend before the next cold front crosses 
the waters by early Monday. Storm conditions are expected at times 
through this evening, while gales persist through midday Friday. 
Additional Small Craft Advisories will likely be needed through 
midday Saturday, and again Sunday evening into Monday.
