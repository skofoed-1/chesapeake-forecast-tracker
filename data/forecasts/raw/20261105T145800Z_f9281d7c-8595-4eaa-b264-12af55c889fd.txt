.SYNOPSIS FOR THE TIDAL POTOMAC AND MD PORTION OF THE CHESAPEAKE BAY...

A cold front approaches from the west today before pushing across 
the waters Friday. Southerly channeling will produce a period of 
Small Craft Advisory conditions for most of the waters through this 
evening. Additional Advisory conditions appear likely Saturday into 
Sunday behind the cold front. Weak high pressure returns this 
weekend before another potent front crosses Monday leading to 
another brief period of Advisory level winds.
