A cold front will move offshore this evening, with strong high 
pressure building to the north through the middle of the week. A 
coastal low pressure area will approach Wednesday night, then move 
over or just east of the region Thursday. The low will pull away by 
Friday. Small Craft Advisories will likely be needed through
